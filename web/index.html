METHODOLOGY

Fast by construction.
Honest about the approximation.

Basis Lab requests venue OHLC bars—not trades—and joins only equal opening timestamps. The close is an exact same-field ratio. Intrabar high and low form a conservative envelope from opposing source extremes, which may not have happened simultaneously.
